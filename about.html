About Me

Hi, I'm Andrew Haugen. I'm currently a Marketing student at Iowa State University and plan to graduate in Fall 2026. I created this website to share more about my background, projects, and goals for the future.

My interests and career plans cover two different areas. My education has given me the opportunity to learn more about marketing, branding, content, and communication, while my long-term plans also include pursuing training in emergency services.

Education

I attend Iowa State University, where I am studying Marketing. Through my coursework, I have gained experience working with topics such as branding, social media content, marketing strategy, communication, and web development.

Projects like the ones featured on this website have allowed me to take concepts from class and apply them to actual designs and marketing ideas.

Work Experience

I currently work as a Sales Associate at Best Buy. Working in sales has given me experience communicating with customers, understanding their needs, and helping them find products that fit what they are looking for.

The job has also given me a chance to see marketing and customer behavior from a practical perspective. Every customer has different priorities, which makes listening and communicating clearly important.

What's Next?

After graduating from Iowa State University in Fall 2026, I plan to attend DMACC in Ankeny, Iowa for EMT and Firefighter training.

I am interested in continuing to build the communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills I have developed through school and work while preparing for this next step.
