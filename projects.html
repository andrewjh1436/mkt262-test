My Projects

This page highlights some of the marketing and design projects I have completed. These projects gave me the opportunity to work with branding, visual design, social media strategy, and content planning.

Red Bull Brand Board

For this project, I created a visual brand board for Red Bull. I focused on the brand's energetic and bold personality by combining its recognizable colors, typography, product lineup, and extreme sports imagery. The goal was to create a consistent visual direction that represents the excitement and adventurous image associated with Red Bull.

What I Learned

This project helped me better understand how colors, fonts, photography, and other visual elements work together to create a consistent brand identity. I also gained more experience using Canva to organize several different design elements into one cohesive layout.

Red Bull Social Media Content Plan

For this project, I developed a social media content plan for Red Bull. The plan included content focused on people, the Red Bull lifestyle, and Red Bull products. Each post idea was connected to a specific marketing goal and social media platform, including Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.

What I Learned

This project helped me understand how brands can use different types of content to accomplish different marketing goals. I also learned how the choice of platform, audience, message, and content format can affect the way a social media campaign is planned.
